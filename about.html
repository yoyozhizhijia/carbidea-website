About Carbidea

A sourcing partner for tungsten carbide components — built for equipment makers and engineers who need custom parts without the runaround.

What we do

Carbidea sits between you and the factory floor. You send a drawing — or a photo, if that's all you have. We pick the right grade, quote it clearly, and manage production, QC and delivery.

Our manufacturing partner has made cemented carbide since 1994. That history matters: it means proven process control, stable grades, and people who have seen the failure modes before.

Who we work with: small and mid-size equipment makers, and engineers validating new designs (NPI). We take the small-batch custom work that big carbide suppliers don't want — and that's exactly where we do our best work.

Small batches are our normal, not a favour. A single prototype part is a fine place to start.
