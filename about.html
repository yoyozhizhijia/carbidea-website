About Carbidea

A sourcing partner for tungsten carbide components — built for equipment makers and engineers who need custom parts without the runaround.

What we do

Carbidea — carbide + idea. Your carbide idea, we engineer.

Carbidea sits between you and the factory floor. You send a drawing — or a photo, if that's all you have. We pick the right grade, quote it clearly, and manage production, QC and delivery.

Our manufacturing partner has made cemented carbide since 1994. That history matters: it means proven process control, stable grades, and people who have seen the failure modes before.

Who we work with: small and mid-size equipment makers, and engineers validating new designs (NPI). We take the small-batch custom work that big carbide suppliers don't want — and that's exactly where we do our best work.

Small batches are our normal, not a favour. A single prototype part is a fine place to start.

A sourcing team built around your parts

Carbidea is an agile sourcing team for custom tungsten carbide parts. We are based where the carbide is made — our in-house QA works in the carbide manufacturing belt, next door to the partner mills that produce your parts.

Every order runs as a small project team:

  • Account — one dedicated contact who knows your drawings and your deadlines. You ask, and the same person answers, every time.
  • Engineering review — every drawing is checked for geometry, grade and tolerances before we confirm anything.
  • In-house QA, at the source — hardness and dimension checks at the mill before anything ships. Because we are on the ground, problems get caught at the press and the sintering furnace — not after a month in transit.
  • Logistics — export, customs and delivery, with active follow-up until the part is in service.

Each partner mill brings its own strengths — in material, in tolerances, in delivery precision — and we match your part to the plant that fits it best. Our partner mills have made cemented carbide since 1994 and are ISO 9001:2015 certified; for capacity, special grades or pricing we coordinate across a network of specialist carbide plants, so you get the mill that fits the order, not just the one we happened to start with.

We are a small team by design: fast decisions, direct answers, one accountable point of contact. That is the sourcing-partner model — and it is why small-batch custom work is our normal, not a favour.
