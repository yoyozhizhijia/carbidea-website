Products

Custom tungsten carbide components, made to drawing. Small batches are our normal work — from a single prototype to a short series.

Valve & Pump Wear Parts

Seats, seat rings, balls, sealing rings and valve cores for high-pressure-drop service. We use high-toughness grades (YG15C class) that hold up to impact and erosion, which is where standard hard grades fail early.

  • High-pressure-drop valves: seat rings, valve cores, balls
  • Pumps and compressors: sealing rings, sleeves, pistons
  • Special applications: coal gasification lock valves, high-temperature service
Typical grades — YG15, YG15C, YG20
QC — hardness + dimension check
Batch — prototype to short series
