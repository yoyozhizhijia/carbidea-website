Carbidea — carbide + idea. Your carbide idea, we engineer.

If you need non-standard tungsten carbide parts — a seal ring with an unusual bore, a wear plate with tight tolerances, a custom shape no catalog offers — you know the real bottleneck isn't price. It's engineering communication.

Carbidea is a tungsten carbide sourcing studio. We read your drawing, translate it into exact grades and tolerances, match it to the right workshop among our partner network, and manage quality and delivery from prototype to small batch. One drawing in — one accountable partner out.

Founded by industry professionals with deep backgrounds in cemented carbide sales, sourcing and quality, we work across pump & valve, mold & die, petrochemical, polysilicon, stud pins, custom shapes and non-magnetic alloys. Beyond shapes, we engineer special grades — non-magnetic and corrosion-resistant alloys — built for demanding environments: high temperature, high pressure, aggressive media, strong magnetic fields.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

No fixed MOQ. We work from prototype and sample orders up to small-batch production — that's exactly why we exist.

What do you need to provide for a quote?

A drawing (PDF, DXF, DWG or STEP) is ideal. If you don't have one, describe the part, application and quantity. You'll have a quote within 1-2 business days.

What are your lead times?

Sample parts typically 2-3 weeks; small-batch production 3-5 weeks depending on complexity. We confirm exact timing with your quote.

How do payments work?

We work on a deposit-before-production basis (typically 50% deposit, balance before shipment). Payment via bank transfer — invoiced with a proforma invoice.

Can I get samples first?

Yes. Small test blocks are provided free of charge — you only cover the shipping cost. For fully machined sample parts, the sample fee applies.

Do you protect my drawings and IP?

Absolutely. Drawings are used solely for quoting and manufacturing. We sign NDA agreements on request, and drawings are never shared outside the workshop selected for your order.
